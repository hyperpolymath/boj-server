# Audit: FFI unsafe blocks (boj-server)

**Auditor**: Jonathan D.A. Jewell  
**Date**: 2026-05-26  
**Scope**: panic-attack assail Critical/High UnsafeCode/UnsafeFFI findings under `ffi/zig/src/`.  
**Registry**: `audits/assail-classifications.a2ml`.

## Rationale

Zig FFI bridge to boj-server's Idris2 backend (cartridge runtime, federation, etc.). Each unsafe pointer cast sits at the Zig→C ABI boundary and is required by Zig to call into `libboj-server`.

This classification is **separate from** the class-J primitive axioms tracked in the backend-assurance harness; those concern the Idris2 trusted base, not the Zig FFI layer.

Refs hyperpolymath/panic-attack#32.
